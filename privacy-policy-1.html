PRIVACY POLICY

Voice Recording AI Guide

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Voice Recording AI Guide Android app (the “App”) handles information. The App is an unofficial guide that helps people set up and use AI voice recorders. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any device manufacturer or brand.

By using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information we do not collect

  • The App has no accounts, sign-in, or registration.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • The App does not record audio and does not access your microphone, contacts, photos, or files. The “recording demo” on the Home screen is a visual simulation only.
  • We do not run our own servers that store personal data about you.

3. Information stored on your device

The App saves a few simple settings locally on your device, such as your selected language chip, switch positions, and checklist progress. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use certain information to serve and measure ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system version, app version, language)
  • Ad interactions (views, clicks, and similar events)
  • Diagnostics and performance data

The ad formats used are app-open ads, interstitial ads, and banner ads. Google’s handling of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region that requires it, the App shows a consent form (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and ads are loaded only as your choices allow.

5. Links and the in-app browser

Some screens open web pages (for example, shopping searches or reference articles) inside the App using an in-app browser. Those websites are run by third parties, may use cookies or similar technologies, and have their own privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for their content or practices. We do not read or store what you do on those pages.

6. Internet connection check

The App checks whether your device is online so it can load ads and web pages. This check happens on your device and does not send personal information to us.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the App is Google, through AdMob, as described in section 4.

9. Data retention and security

We do not keep personal data about you. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google’s own policies. Locally saved settings remain on your device until you delete them. We rely on the security features of Android and use encrypted (HTTPS) connections for web content.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent for its processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are best directed to Google through your Google Account and ad settings. You can also contact us at the address below and we will do our best to help.

11. Recording laws notice

The App contains general information about recording conversations. It is for information only and is not legal advice. Laws on recording calls and meetings differ by country and state, so always get the consent required where you are before you record anyone.

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
